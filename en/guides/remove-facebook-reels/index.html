Remove Reels from Facebook

Facebook has a Feeds tab that shows your friends and nothing else. Almost nobody knows it exists, and it never stays selected.

Updated 2026-09-172 min read

Facebook is a special case: a Friends-only feed genuinely exists, and it does exactly what you'd want. It's just filed where nobody looks.

It's called Feeds, and it lives in the menu rather than the main bar. It shows posts from your friends, in chronological order, with no suggestions and no Reels.

The Feeds tab, and why it isn't enough

1

Find it

In the app menu, look for Feeds. It offers several views, one of them Friends: only posts from people you actually added.

2

Pin it

On some versions it can be added to your shortcuts. That cuts the number of taps; it doesn't change what opens when you tap the Facebook icon.

And that's the catch: Facebook doesn't remember your choice. The app always opens on the algorithmic feed, with its Reels, its suggested groups and its pages you never liked. The right feed exists, but you have to ask for it every single time.

The setting you're looking for exists. It just never stays ticked.

What Slowcial strips on Facebook

The Reels tab in the header, the Reels shelves wedged into the feed, and sponsored posts. The last audit counted the tab, two in-feed shelves and one sponsored post — all neutralised.

  • Messenger and your conversations are untouched
  • Your groups stay reachable
  • Events and the pages you follow don't move
A known flaw, stated plainly

On Facebook, the hidden Reels tab sometimes leaves a grey rectangle in the header. It's cosmetic, it's logged in our own audit, and it's being fixed — you may as well know before you install.

Frequently asked questions

Does Facebook have a feed without Reels?

Yes: the Feeds tab, Friends view, shows only posts from your friends, with no suggestions and no Reels. Its flaw is that it doesn't stay selected — the app always reopens on the algorithmic feed.

Can you delete the Reels tab on Facebook?

No app setting allows it. It can, however, be stripped at display time by a filtering tool.

Is Messenger affected?

No. Filtering applies to Facebook's pages; messages, whether in the app or in Messenger, are not touched.
