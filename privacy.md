# Privacy Policy — Slowcial

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

Slowcial helps you use social networks more calmly by hiding the content designed
to keep you scrolling. This page explains exactly what data exists, where it lives,
and what happens to it.

In short: **we collect nothing about you.** Slowcial has no server, no account and
no analytics. The only thing that can leave your phone is an anonymous crash
report — and you can switch it off.

## What Slowcial stores — on your device only

The following is stored in the app's private storage, on your device. It is never
transmitted, to us or to anyone else:

- the nickname you choose (optional — you can stay anonymous);
- your preferences: followed networks, active filters, theme, language;
- your answers to the setup questions (estimated daily usage, goal);
- time spent in the app, per day and per network, kept for a rolling 90 days, which
  powers the "Stats" screen.

All of it disappears when you use "Start over" in Settings, or uninstall the app.

## What happens when you open a network

When you open Instagram, YouTube or another network from Slowcial, the page is
displayed in an embedded web view, and **you sign in directly with that network**.
Three consequences:

1. Your session (cookies, site data) is stored locally by that web view, just like
   in a browser. Slowcial does not read, copy or transmit your credentials or the
   content shown.
2. The network you visit receives the data it normally receives when you visit it,
   and applies **its own** privacy policy, over which we have no control.
3. The "Reset" button in each network's menu clears that site's cookies and data,
   and signs you out.

Slowcial only acts on how the page is displayed, on your phone. No content is sent
elsewhere, analysed remotely, or stored by us.

## Subscription

If you subscribe to Slowcial Premium, the purchase is handled by Apple. We never
see your payment method, your Apple ID or your address. We only receive the fact
that this device has an active subscription.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes, a technical report may be sent to **Sentry** (Functional
Software, Inc.) to help us fix it. It contains where in the code it broke, the
device model and the iOS version.

It contains **no nickname, no settings and none of the pages you visited**: app
logs and addresses opened in the web view are explicitly dropped before sending,
and your IP address is not retained.

You can turn this off in **Settings → Privacy → Crash reports**.
See also: https://sentry.io/privacy/

## Daily reminder

If you enable it, Slowcial shows **one** notification a day, at the time you
choose. It is scheduled by your phone: there is no push server, no notification
token sent anywhere, and therefore no way for us to know whether you received or
opened it. Turn it off in the app's settings, or in iOS settings.

## What Slowcial does not do

- No account, no sign-up, no email address requested.
- No analytics, no advertising tracker, no profiling.
- No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell.
- No access to your messages, contacts, location or photos.

## Children

Slowcial is not intended for children under 13 and knowingly collects no data
about them.

## Your rights

Since nothing leaves your phone, you stay in direct control: "Start over" erases
your Slowcial data, "Reset" erases a network's data, uninstalling erases
everything. We hold no copy, so there is no access or deletion request to make to
us.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new update date.
A change that would involve collecting data would be announced inside the app
before taking effect.
